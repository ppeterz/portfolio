Optimizing power efficiency in IoT devices

Power efficiency is the silent killer of IoT projects. A device that drains its battery becomes a maintenance job. Here's how to build IoT systems that last in the real world.

Choose hardware that can sleep

Not all microcontrollers are equal. Look for chips with dependable deep-sleep modes and the ability to shut peripherals down. The ESP32-C3, for example, drops to about 5 µA in deep sleep while keeping its real-time clock running.

// ESP32: sleep, then wake every 30 seconds
esp_sleep_enable_timer_wakeup(30 * 1000000ULL);  // microseconds
esp_deep_sleep_start();
Tips
  • Pick sensors with a standby current under 1 µA.
  • Use voltage regulators that stay efficient at light loads.
  • Consider energy harvesting for outdoor deployments.

Keep the radio off

Wireless radios are the hungriest part of most devices. The goal is the least radio-on time that still gives a reliable link: batch your data, use efficient protocols, and retry with care.

Instead ofStreaming a reading every second over Wi-Fi, which can draw 200 mA or more the whole time.
Do thisCollect 10 minutes of data, send it in one burst and sleep for the rest. Average draw: 2 to 5 mA.

Frontend design principles for engineers

Great frontend isn't only about pretty interfaces. It's about experiences people actually want to use. Here's what every engineer should know.

Clarity above all

Every interface should pass the five-second test: can a new user tell what to do within five seconds? Use consistent type, a clear hierarchy and deliberate white space.

ClutteredEverything competes, so nothing leads.
CleanOne heading, one line of context, one action.

Performance is UX

Speed shapes how people feel about a product and how it performs as a business. A 100 ms delay can cut conversions by 7%. Watch the Core Web Vitals and how fast the page feels, not just how fast it is.

Load less
  • Compress and lazy-load images
  • Minify CSS and JS
  • Serve static files from a CDN
Load smarter
  • Split code by route
  • Speed up first paint
  • Cut time to interactive
Measure
  • Lighthouse CI
  • Web Vitals in the field
  • Error tracking

Accessibility is non-negotiable

About one in six people worldwide lives with a disability. Accessible design is the right thing to do, it's good business, and it usually makes the product easier for everyone.

// A button that says what it does, and what it's doing
<button
  aria-label="Add item to cart"
  onClick={handleAddToCart}
  disabled={loading}
>
  {loading ? 'Adding…' : 'Add to cart'}
</button>

From idea to MVP in 30 days

Speed beats perfection early on. Here's a tested playbook for shipping an MVP that checks your core assumptions fast.

Week 1: define and validate the problem

Start with the problem, not the solution. If you can't state the problem in one sentence, you're not ready to build.

Target user struggles with specific problem when situation, which causes impact.

Days 1 to 3: research
  • Interview 10 potential users
  • Study competing solutions
  • Map the pain points in their day
  • Define how you'll measure success
Days 4 to 7: focus
  • Write the one-sentence problem
  • Find your riskiest assumption
  • Design a test for it
  • Sketch wireframes

Weeks 2 and 3: build the minimum

Cut features without mercy. Your MVP should test the riskiest assumption with the least work.

Frontend
  • Next.js and Tailwind
  • Deploy on Vercel
  • Framer Motion
Backend
  • Supabase or Firebase
  • Stripe for payments
  • Resend for email
Analytics
  • PostHog events
  • Google Analytics
  • Hotjar heatmaps
Common mistakes
  • Building too many features
  • Polishing the design before anyone has validated the idea
  • Setting up complex infrastructure
  • Ignoring early user feedback

Week 4: launch and learn

Launch to a small, relevant audience. The goal isn't viral growth. It's learning what's true.

  • Activation rateAim for 30%
  • Feature usageThe core feature
  • Time to valueUnder 5 minutes

Hear from users three ways: an in-app feedback widget, short interview calls, and behaviour analytics.
