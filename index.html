04 / 04HG-BELLFirmware 1.0.0

HoneyGold school bell

Rings a school's weekly timetable on its own, and keeps time through power cuts.

MCU
ESP8266 (NodeMCU)
Clock
DS3231 RTC with coin-cell backup
Outputs
Relay to the bell, buzzer, 16×2 I²C LCD
Schedule
64 weekly bell events, 24 holidays
Network
LAN-only dashboard, setup hotspot, OTA
  1. Runs entirely on the school's network and never calls out to the internet. Time comes from the RTC, not NTP.
  2. One button runs it without a phone: hold it 1.5 s for an emergency ring.
  3. Built to fail safe: a 180 s hard cutoff on the relay, a hardware watchdog, and safe mode after three bad boots.
